### **Chapter 1: Number Systems**

**1.** Which of the following is a rational number?
(a) $1 + \sqrt{3}$
(b) $\pi$
(c) $2\sqrt{3}$
(d) 0
**Correct Answer:** (d)

(a) 0
(b) $-4.3$
(c) $-3.4$
(d) $1.101100110001 \dots$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{2}{1}$
(c) $\frac{5}{6}$ and $\frac{7}{6}$
(d) $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{4}{3}$

(a) a rational number
